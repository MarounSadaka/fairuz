How to bring in the WhatsApp voices
1. Press “📋 Copy WhatsApp message” next to a point and send it to that classmate. It has the text to read and recording tips (in Arabic).
2. When the voice note arrives, get it onto this laptop: WhatsApp Desktop / web.whatsapp.com, or on the phone long-press the voice note → Share → send it to yourself by email or Drive.
3. Drag the file (usually .opus, .ogg or .m4a) onto that point’s row, or press “🎙 Choose voice”.
4. Each voice is cleaned automatically: silence trimmed from both ends, loudness evened out, rumble removed.
5. When everything is in, press “💾 Save final version”. You get one file, Fairuz_final.html, holding every voice, the photo, the music and these settings. Take that file to class. It runs offline.
